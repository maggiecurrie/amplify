# Leader Voice Engine

**Human, story-led content at scale, with AI-enabled systems.**

Every company is sitting on great content: webinars, blogs, keynotes, reports. And every leader *should* be showing up on LinkedIn. This system connects the two.

> AI does the heavy lifting. Leaders bring the story. No post goes live until a human makes it their own.

---

## The problem

- Great content gets published once, then forgotten.
- Leaders know they should post, but don't have the time.
- Ghostwritten or AI-written posts often sound generic and erode trust.

## How it works

1. **Collect:** Gather existing content (webinars, blogs, podcasts, keynotes).
2. **Extract:** AI surfaces the strongest quotes and ideas. A human picks the keepers.
3. **Bank:** Approved quotes go into a quote bank, tagged by leader, theme, and source.
4. **Draft:** AI turns a quote into a LinkedIn post using that leader's voice profile.
5. **Humanize:** The leader adds a personal story, opinion, or detail. This is what makes it theirs.
6. **Approve & post:** The leader signs off. The quote is marked as used.

## Who does what

| Humans lead | AI powers |
|---|---|
| Choosing what matters | Pulling quotes from long content |
| Adding real stories and opinions | Drafting posts in each leader's voice |
| The final call | Speed, volume, and repurposing |

## Example: Ledgerly (fictional)

To show the system end to end, this repository uses a **fictional fintech company**, Ledgerly, with three made-up leaders:

- **Maya Chen**, CEO
- **David Okafor**, Chief Revenue Officer
- **Priya Shah**, Chief Risk & Compliance Officer

One webinar becomes posts for all three, each in their own voice.

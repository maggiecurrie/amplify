# Human edit checklist

Run this on every draft before it goes to the leader for approval. If any answer is "no", the post isn't ready.

## Truth

- [ ] Every fact, number, and claim is true and checked
- [ ] The quote is word for word what the leader said
- [ ] The story is real, and told the way the leader told it

## Human

- [ ] The **[STORY GAP]** has been filled with a real moment from the leader
- [ ] There's at least one detail no AI could have invented (a name, a place, a line someone said)
- [ ] It has an opinion, not just information

## Voice

- [ ] Read it aloud: it sounds like this leader on a good day
- [ ] Nothing from their "would never say" list
- [ ] Length, emojis, hashtags, and ending match their voice profile

## Remove the AI fingerprints

- [ ] No "In today's fast-paced world", "game-changer", "Let's dive in", or "Here's the thing nobody tells you"
- [ ] No sentences that are three adjectives long
- [ ] No tidy, symmetrical lists where a sentence would do
- [ ] The ending isn't a generic "What do you think?"

## Story

- [ ] **Hook:** the first line earns the second
- [ ] **Tension:** there's a problem, a surprise, or a stake
- [ ] **Turn:** the reader leaves with something useful or a new way of seeing it

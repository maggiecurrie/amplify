# Leader approval checklist

A short checklist for the leader. It should take under two minutes.

## Before you approve, ask yourself:

- [ ] **Is this true?** Would I stand behind every word if someone asked me about it?
- [ ] **Is this me?** Would my team recognize my voice?
- [ ] **Is the story right?** Did it happen the way it's told?
- [ ] **Am I comfortable?** Would I be happy if a customer, employee, or competitor read this?
- [ ] **Do I want to reply to comments on it?** If not, it's probably not your post yet.

## Then choose one:

- ✅ **Approve:** post as is
- ✏️ **Approve with edits:** make your changes, and they'll go live as you wrote them
- 🔁 **Not yet:** tell us what's off, and we'll rework it
- ❌ **Skip:** no explanation needed

*The leader always has the final say. Always.*

# Worked example: Ledgerly

*Ledgerly is a fictional fintech company that helps small businesses manage cash flow and get paid faster. Everything here is invented to show the system working end to end.*

## From one webinar to a published post

| Step | What happened | See it |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Collect** | The team gathered a 30-minute webinar, two blog posts, and a keynote. | [Webinar transcript](source-content/webinar-cash-flow-is-a-story.md) |
| **2. Extract** | AI suggested 11 candidate quotes from the webinar. The content lead kept 5. | [Extract prompt](../../prompts/01-extract-quotes.md) |
| **3. Bank** | The 5 quotes went into the quote bank, tagged and marked Approved. | [Quote bank](../../quote-bank/quotes.csv) |
| **4. Draft** | Quote Q001 + Maya's voice profile → AI first draft, with a [STORY GAP]. | [Maya's voice profile](../../voice-profiles/maya-chen.md) |
| **5. Humanize** | Maya answered 3 story questions in a 2-minute voice note. Her story went in. | [Story questions prompt](../../prompts/03-story-questions.md) |
| **6. Approve** | Maya made two small edits and approved it. Q001 was marked Used. | [The post: AI draft vs. final](posts/maya-chen-q001.md) |

## The human difference

Open **[Maya's post](posts/maya-chen-q001.md)**, then click **History** at the top of the file. You'll see two versions:

1. **AI first draft:** competent, on-voice, but generic, with a gap where the story should be.
2. **Human final:** Maya's real story, her words, her edits.

Click into the second version to see the side-by-side, red-and-green comparison of exactly what changed.

# Human Edit showcase

Each post here is saved twice in its history: first the **AI draft**, then the **human final**. That way anyone can see exactly what the human added.

## How to see the difference

1. Open a post, for example [maya-chen-q001.md](maya-chen-q001.md).
2. Click **History** (top right of the file).
3. Click the most recent change, "Human edit: …".
4. Red lines are what the AI wrote. Green lines are what the human changed it to.

How it works

Six steps. Humans lead the ones that matter.

  1. Collect Human

    Gather existing content: webinars, podcasts, keynotes, blogs, reports.

  2. Extract AIHuman

    AI surfaces the strongest quotes. A human keeps only the true, specific ones.

  3. Bank Human

    Approved quotes go into a quote bank, tagged by leader, theme, and source.

  4. Draft AI

    AI drafts a post in the leader's voice and leaves a marked story gap on purpose.

  5. Humanize Human

    The leader answers three quick questions in a voice note. Their real story fills the gap.

  6. Approve & post Human

    The leader signs off, every time. The quote is marked as used.

Humans lead

  • Deciding what matters
  • Real stories, opinions, and lived detail
  • Judgment on tone, truth, and timing
  • The final call

AI powers

  • Pulling quotes from long content
  • First drafts in each leader's voice
  • Variations and repurposing
  • Speed and volume
The human difference

Same quote. Same idea. Now it's hers.

An example from Ledgerly, a fictional fintech company. On the left is the AI first draft for its CEO, Maya Chen. On the right is the post she actually published after adding her own story.

AI first draft

Cash flow isn't a spreadsheet problem.

It's a sleep-at-night problem.

When small business owners talk about money, they rarely start with numbers. They start with how it feels: the stress, the uncertainty, the late nights.

[STORY GAP]: Maya, add a real moment here.

That's why we believe financial tools should do more than save time. They should give people peace of mind.

What does cash flow stress look like in your business?

Published by Maya

My dad ran a 30-seat restaurant for 22 years.

I don't remember him worrying about the food. I remember him at the kitchen table after midnight, with a calculator and a shoebox of receipts, working out who he could pay that week.

When I talk to small business owners now, they describe it the same way. Not in numbers. In moments.

Cash flow isn't a spreadsheet problem. It's a sleep-at-night problem.

That's the whole reason Ledgerly exists. Not to make finance faster, but to give people their evenings back.

My dad would have liked that.

Creatives stay at the centre

Humans make it. Humans amplify it. AI just clears the way.

A lot of AI tools try to replace the people who make content. Amplify does the opposite. The creative work stays human, and AI is used to help more humans share it.

Step 1

Designers, videographers, writers, and social teams create the stories, videos, and campaigns, with all the craft and judgment that takes.

How this scales

This is the demo. Here's how it runs at a real company.

This sample library is filled in by hand so you can see the experience. At a company publishing every week, the same system grows on its own, and people stay in charge of what goes out.

01 · AI organizes

Drop it in, AI does the prep

Add a video, case study, webinar, or report, and AI reads the transcript or text, pulls the strongest quotes, tags the themes, and drafts captions for each role.

02 · Humans approve

The social team signs off

Nothing goes into the library until someone on the content team checks the quotes are exact, the facts are right, and every caption leaves room for a real story.

03 · More channels

Beyond LinkedIn

The same kit adapts to Instagram, X, Threads, email signatures, and newsletters, each with captions and image sizes that fit the platform.

04 · Comes to the team

Delivered where people work

A weekly kit lands in Slack or Teams on Monday, so sharing becomes a five-minute habit, not another tool to remember.

05 · Connected

Pulls from where content lives

New content flows in from YouTube, the blog, the CMS, or Google Drive automatically, so the library is always current.

06 · Measured

See what goes further

Track which pieces get shared most, by which teams, and what they add, so the content team learns what really resonates.

What never changes: AI does the heavy lifting, and a person always adds the story.

Before you post

A few simple rules.

Do

  • Add your own sentence, even a short one
  • Tag the company, and customers if they've agreed
  • Reply to comments within a day
  • Spread posts across the week, so we don't all post at once

Don't

  • Post the caption word for word
  • Change anyone's quotes or numbers
  • Promise results to other customers
  • Share anything that isn't in the kit

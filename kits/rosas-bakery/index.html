Rosa's Bakery × Ledgerly

Rosa Delgado runs a 12-person bakery that supplies cafés across the city. Her wholesale customers paid on average 64 days late, so she spent Sunday nights chasing invoices instead of being with her family.

With Ledgerly's automatic reminders and early payment options, she now gets paid in 9 days, and has her Sundays back.

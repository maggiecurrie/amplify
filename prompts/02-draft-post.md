# Prompt 2: Draft a LinkedIn post

**Use in:** Step 4 (Draft)
**Input:** One approved quote + the leader's voice profile
**Output:** A first draft with a marked gap for the leader's own story

---

## The prompt

```
You are drafting a LinkedIn post for a company leader. This is a FIRST
DRAFT. The leader will add their own story and approve the final version.

Write in the voice described in the profile below. Follow its length,
emoji, hashtag, and ending preferences exactly. Avoid every phrase on the
"would never say" list.

Build the post around this quote. The quote can appear word for word, or
the post can expand on the idea behind it.

Structure:
1. Hook: a first line that makes someone stop scrolling. No questions
   as hooks unless the profile says they use them.
2. Tension: the problem, the surprise, or the thing most people get wrong.
3. [STORY GAP]: leave a clearly marked placeholder where the leader should
   add a real moment from their own experience. Suggest 2 possible stories
   from their profile, but do NOT write the story for them.
4. Turn: the insight or lesson.
5. Ending, in the style the profile describes.

Rules:
- Do not invent facts, numbers, customers, or experiences.
- No corporate jargon, no hype, no "In today's fast-paced world."
- Write how this person talks, not how a company writes.

After the draft, add:
- 2 alternative hooks
- One sentence on what the leader's story needs to do for this post to land

QUOTE:
[paste quote]

VOICE PROFILE:
[paste voice profile]
```

## Why it's built this way

- **The [STORY GAP]** is the most important design choice in the whole system. The AI deliberately does *not* fill it, so every post needs a real human moment before it can go live.
- **"Do not invent"** protects the leader from saying something untrue in public.
- **Alternative hooks** give the human options to choose from rather than one take to accept.

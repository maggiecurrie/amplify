# Prompt 3: Story questions for the leader

**Use in:** Step 5 (Humanize)
**Input:** The draft post with its [STORY GAP]
**Output:** 3 short questions to send the leader, so they can fill the gap in a quick voice note

---

## The prompt

```
Below is a draft LinkedIn post with a [STORY GAP]. Write 3 short questions
to send to the leader so they can fill that gap with a real moment from
their own experience.

The questions should:
- Be answerable in a 2-minute voice note, with no writing needed
- Ask for a specific moment (when, where, who, what was said), not an opinion
- Feel like a curious colleague asking, not an interview
- Each come at the story from a different angle

Then write a one-line message the content lead can send with the questions,
in a friendly, low-pressure tone.

DRAFT POST:
[paste draft]
```

## Example questions it should produce

- "When's the last time you saw a customer deal with this? What happened?"
- "What did you believe about this five years ago that you don't now?"
- "Is there a moment from early Ledgerly days that this reminds you of?"

## Why it's built this way

Leaders are busy, but almost everyone can *tell* a story out loud. This step turns "write a LinkedIn post" (which they'll put off) into "answer a quick question" (which takes two minutes). The content lead does the rest.

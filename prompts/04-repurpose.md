# Prompt 4: Repurpose a published post

**Use in:** After Step 6, to get more value from a post that worked
**Input:** A final, leader-approved post
**Output:** Variations for other formats, all keeping the leader's story intact

---

## The prompt

```
Below is a LinkedIn post that the leader wrote and approved. It includes a
personal story that is the heart of the post.

Create these variations. Keep the leader's story and their exact words for
it intact. You may shorten around it, but do not change what happened.

1. A 3-slide carousel outline (one line of text per slide + a closing slide)
2. A 40-word version for a newsletter "From our leaders" section
3. A 30-second video script the leader could record on their phone
4. 2 comment-starter questions the leader could post as the first comment

Write in the same voice as the original post.

FINAL POST:
[paste post]
```

## Why it's built this way

Once a leader has shared a real story, it deserves more than one appearance. This multiplies the reach of the human part without diluting it, because the story stays theirs, word for word.

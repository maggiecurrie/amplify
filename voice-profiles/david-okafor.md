# Voice profile: David Okafor, Chief Revenue Officer

*Fictional leader at Ledgerly, an invented fintech company.*

## Snapshot

- **Role:** Chief Revenue Officer
- **Why people listen:** He's spent 15 years selling to small businesses and talks like someone who still takes the calls.
- **His audience:** Sales leaders, revenue teams, small business owners

## How he sounds

- **Three words:** Direct, practical, generous
- **Sentence style:** Short. Punchy. Lists and numbers. Gets to the point in the first line.
- **Energy:** Coach on the sidelines: encouraging, but no fluff.
- **He often says:** "Here's the thing.", "Simple test:", "Steal this."
- **He would never say:** "circle back", "leverage", "thought leadership", anything vague

## What he cares about

- **Topics he owns:** Customer-first selling, listening over pitching, building revenue teams
- **Opinions he'll defend:** Discovery beats demos. Quota should never outrank the customer's best interest.
- **Topics to avoid:** Specific revenue numbers, client names

## His stories

1. Losing his biggest deal early in his career because he talked for 40 minutes straight.
2. A bakery owner who explained Ledgerly's value better than his own pitch deck.
3. Riding along with a new rep and realizing the training was the problem.

## Formatting preferences

- **Length:** 80–150 words
- **Emojis:** Occasionally, for list markers
- **Hashtags:** One, at most
- **How he ends a post:** A practical takeaway or a "try this" challenge

## Approval

- **Who approves:** David, directly
- **Turnaround:** 24 hours

# Voice profile: Maya Chen, CEO

*Fictional leader at Ledgerly, an invented fintech company.*

## Snapshot

- **Role:** Co-founder and CEO
- **Why people listen:** She built Ledgerly for people like her own family, and it shows.
- **Her audience:** Small business owners, founders, fintech peers, future hires

## How she sounds

- **Three words:** Warm, visionary, grounded
- **Sentence style:** Starts with a moment or image, then zooms out to the bigger point. Mixes short lines with one longer, reflective one.
- **Energy:** Calm conviction. Never hype.
- **She often says:** "Here's what I keep coming back to…", "the people behind the numbers"
- **She would never say:** "disrupt", "synergy", "we're crushing it", anything that sounds like a press release

## What she cares about

- **Topics she owns:** Why small businesses matter, the human cost of cash flow stress, building a company with heart
- **Opinions she'll defend:** Financial tools should reduce anxiety, not just save time. Small businesses deserve the same tools as big ones.
- **Topics to avoid:** Fundraising brags, competitor jabs, politics

## Her stories

1. Her dad doing the books for his restaurant at the kitchen table after midnight.
2. The first customer who emailed to say she'd taken a weekend off for the first time in two years.
3. Almost shutting down in year two, and the team meeting that changed her mind.

## Formatting preferences

- **Length:** 120–200 words
- **Emojis:** None, or one at most
- **Hashtags:** None
- **How she ends a post:** A reflective question to the reader

## Approval

- **Who approves:** Maya, directly
- **Turnaround:** 48 hours

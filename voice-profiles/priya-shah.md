# Voice profile: Priya Shah, Chief Risk & Compliance Officer

*Fictional leader at Ledgerly, an invented fintech company.*

## Snapshot

- **Role:** Chief Risk & Compliance Officer
- **Why people listen:** She makes trust, risk, and regulation feel human and important, not boring.
- **Her audience:** Fintech peers, compliance and risk professionals, customers who want to know their money is safe

## How she sounds

- **Three words:** Thoughtful, steady, clear
- **Sentence style:** Measured. Uses analogies from everyday life to explain complex ideas. Rarely uses exclamation marks.
- **Energy:** The calm person in the room during a crisis.
- **She often says:** "Put simply…", "The question I ask is…", "Trust is built in small moments."
- **She would never say:** Jargon without explaining it, fear-based messaging, "bulletproof", guarantees

## What she cares about

- **Topics she owns:** Trust, security, responsible innovation, compliance as a growth enabler
- **Opinions she'll defend:** Compliance done well speeds a company up. Transparency is a product feature.
- **Topics to avoid:** Specific incidents, regulator relationships, anything that could read as legal advice

## Her stories

1. Explaining encryption to her grandmother using a locked recipe box.
2. The time the team delayed a launch by two weeks to get it right, and why customers never knew.
3. Her first job at a bank, watching a customer's face when a fraud charge was reversed.

## Formatting preferences

- **Length:** 150–220 words
- **Emojis:** None
- **Hashtags:** None
- **How she ends a post:** A clear principle the reader can apply

## Approval

- **Who approves:** Priya, plus a quick legal check for regulatory topics
- **Turnaround:** 72 hours
